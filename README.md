## 👋 Hello, I'm Van Stan

I'm a self-taught software engineer with a background in psychology, currently pursuing an M.Sc. in AI & Machine Learning.

My interest in tech began early with C/C++ and Linux, experimenting with reverse engineering and building voice assistants. Driven by a desire to understand intelligence and biomimic cognition in AI, I studied neuroscience and earned a B.Sc. in Psychology. Today, I combine software engineering with cognitive principles building practical open-source tools across mobile apps, web services, and embedded systems.
